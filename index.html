See the wood—and the trees

PhysicsKG organises a large subject without turning it into either a rigid textbook sequence or an unreadable tangle of links.

Folded modules reveal the main intellectual landscape. Expand one to work with its concepts, or follow carefully typed relationships to see prerequisites, derivations and uses across the atlas.

Answer questions, mark reading progress and keep notes alongside the relevant material.

Personal work remains personal

Local first, portable by choice

Notes, layouts, reading ticks and study-question records stay in your browser by default. You can download them as transparent CSV files in a ZIP archive, or optionally synchronise the same archive through your own Google Drive.
