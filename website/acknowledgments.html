Acknowledgments

PhysicsKG is created by Ian Poole, with AI assistance via OpenAI Codex. It builds on the work of teachers, researchers and open-source contributors.

Teaching sources

The SR material owes much of its overall structure and progression to Leonard Susskind and Art Friedman’s Special Relativity and Classical Field Theory, volume II of The Theoretical Minimum.

David Tong’s General Relativity lectures are a principal mathematical reference for the MATHS and GR content. His quantum field theory lectures also inform parts of the SR field-theory treatment. PhysicsKG’s concept References credit these and other sources, with section details where available.

The teaching examples, artwork and reading-tree presentation are developed for PhysicsKG.

Open-source software

  • MathJax typesets the mathematics in the viewer — Apache License 2.0.
  • vis-network draws the interactive graph — available under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licence.
  • PyVis generates the graph’s HTML foundation and supplies browser helpers — BSD 3-Clause.
  • Bootstrap supplies base styling for the viewer — MIT.
  • Tom Select is included among the legacy bundled assets — Apache License 2.0.

Building and testing

The authoring and build tools use Python, NetworkX, pandas, NumPy, Jinja2 and PyYAML. Automated checks use pytest and Playwright.
