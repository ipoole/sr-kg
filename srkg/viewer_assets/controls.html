Knowledge graph browser

Explore the physics knowledge base through two peer views: the graph shows concept relationships, while the details pane gives the study text for the selected concept.

Navigation and layout

  • Select from the graph, searchable concept list, or concept links.
  • Use browser back and forward to retrace selected concepts.
  • Show, hide, or focus the graph; show, hide, or filter details.
  • Drag the divider to rebalance graph and details space.
  • Move nodes in the full graph; personal layouts survive reload and can be exported or reset. New

Study content

  • Reading modes switch between full, core, maths, context, and practice views.
  • Semantic blocks render as textbook-style callouts, folded notes, and study questions.
  • Preview linked concepts inline without leaving the current concept.
  • Add browser-local notes, then export or import them as CSV.

Graph semantics

  • Use the focus lens in Auto mode to follow the active detail section, or choose arbitrary relation-and-direction traversals in Manual mode. New
  • Derived-from and where-used sections can show immediate links or full trees.
  • Inspect relation colours and meanings from the edge key.
  • Hover edges for MathJax-rendered notes or click them for relationship details.

Maths and graphics

  • MathJax renders equations in details, graph labels, previews, and edge notes.
  • Generated SVG concept graphics appear in details and graph nodes where available.

Modules New

  • Browse authored topic groups and open module-level study pages.
  • Collapse modules into topic nodes, then expand them to reveal their concepts.
  • Inspect summarised boundary links between folded modules and the surrounding graph.
