__PRODUCT_NAME__ quick start

__PRODUCT_TAGLINE__

Explore Special Relativity, General Relativity and supporting mathematics. Modules group related concepts; select one to start reading, or take the guided tutorial below.

Find a topic

  • Select a module or concept to read its Details, or use Search.
  • Use a module’s + to reveal its concepts; − folds it again.
  • Drag empty graph space to pan; pinch or use the wheel to zoom. Fit brings the context into view.

Choose what to see

  • View chooses Graph, Details or Both. On phones, use one pane at a time for more room.
  • Graph chooses Overview, Context + background or Context only.
  • Context chooses the connections to follow; Depth chooses how far. Try Derivation or Foundations, then Fit.

Read and practise

  • Open or fold Details blocks; use Contents to jump between them. Pinch or Ctrl/Cmd-wheel to enlarge Details.
  • Answer Study Questions and use blue checkboxes to mark blocks read.
  • A speech-bubble + adds a personal note.

Keep your work

Notes, reading marks, question progress and personal layouts save in this browser. Tools → Personal data offers export/import and optional Google Drive synchronisation across devices.
