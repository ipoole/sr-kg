Knowledge graph browser

Explore the physics knowledge base through two linked views: the graph shows concept relationships, while the details pane gives the study text for the selected concept.

  • Select a concept from the graph or searchable concept list to update both views.
  • Use the Graph menu to show all concepts, hide the graph, or focus on the selected concept's current context.
  • Use the Details menu to switch between full, core, maths, context, and practice reading modes.
  • The focus lens summarises how the graph focus is being chosen for the active detail section.
  • Derived-from and where-used sections can drive the graph focus, including optional full-tree views.
  • Drag the divider to give more space to the graph or the details pane.
  • Preview linked concepts inline, inspect edge meanings from the edge key, and click edges for relationship details.
  • Add browser-local notes, then export or import them as CSV.
