## Priorities and working rules

Initial SR priorities are simultaneity/length measurements, proper time and the
two-light-pulse system, varied histories and field-action cells, an observer or
gauge comparison, and Noether/flux bookkeeping. Expect about seven to nine body
figures after inspecting the existing artwork, not one per concept. Together with
the three planned MATHS figures this is near the agreed 8–12-figure range.

Keep exposition near 70, with short result statements and meaningful titles.
Preserve all current source/clarification/question IDs, dependencies and tutorial
targets. Do not change kinds merely to include a picture in a reading recipe.
Graphics stay inside `domains/sr/`; reuse assets within that domain where useful,
without requiring MATHS or GR artwork for an SR-only build. No physics text or
assets are changed by this inventory commit.

Read the guide and review full bodies, questions and references before each SR
module; commit each separately, inspect phone/desktop equations and artwork,
then review the complete reading experience and publish tested previews.
