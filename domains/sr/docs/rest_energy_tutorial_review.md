# Rest-energy teaching route

## Audience and objective

For a reader comfortable with elementary SR who still finds mass–energy
equivalence slippery. Explain why rest energy is physical, how it relates to
kinetic energy, and why the mass of a system includes internal energy.

## Audit

The existing route follows four-momentum, four-velocity, proper time, interval,
Lorentz transformations and the SR postulates. It then returns to the rest-frame
limit. This is a useful geometry spine, but it omits the physical identification
of energy and most of the interpretation already present in the mass–energy
concept. The four-momentum components block labels gamma mc-squared as energy
without explaining work, the low-speed limit, or conservation across systems.

Spacetime geometry alone does not establish dynamics. Adopt the massive-particle
momentum law p = gamma m v, with m calibrated by its Newtonian limit. With
three-force F = dp/dt and work rate F dot v, constant m gives
d(gamma mc-squared)/dt = F dot v. This identifies energy changes; integrating
work from rest gives K = (gamma - 1)mc-squared. Mechanical work alone leaves
an additive constant. The conserved relativistic energy–momentum description
of isolated systems fixes how rest energy participates in exchanges. State
that physical input explicitly rather than pretending work alone proves it.

## Content changes

- Strengthen the four-momentum components explanation; add separate blocks on
  work, the Newtonian limit, and the remaining physical input.
- Distinguish total energy, rest energy and kinetic energy in the rest-energy
  concept, using m for a particle and M for a composite system when helpful.
- Develop the heated-box example: energy enters without matter transfer;
  after equilibration the box is again at rest, with mass increased by Q/c².
  Include the container and account for energy that escapes.
- Explain why internal motion contributes even when total momentum vanishes;
  contrast heating with simply boosting the whole box.
- Preserve semantic block and question IDs. Reuse the six existing questions,
  especially the calculation and heated-box checks.
- Keep the derivation edge from rest energy to four-momentum and clarify its
  note. Do not add redundant shortcut prerequisites or a new schema.

## Route

Begin with the puzzle and heated box; distinguish the three energy quantities.
Trace the existing foundations backwards, then reconstruct forwards through
four-velocity, momentum, physical energy, the invariant relation and rest frame.
Return to internal energy, a numerical example and the misconception block.
Finish at Study Questions, inviting exploration to answer rather than recording
an answer or changing progress automatically. Prompts point to authored blocks.

## Boundaries

This route does not prove universal energy conservation from the SR postulates,
derive microscopic heating dynamics, or introduce relativistic mass. Automatic
route generation and tutorial recording remain deferred. Time dilation is the
next content/tutorial pass, after review of this route.
